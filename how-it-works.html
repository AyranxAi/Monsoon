How It Works

An open airway,
by design.

Conventional ventilation seals the airway and cycles large tidal volumes. Monsoon 4 takes the opposite approach — a narrow, high-velocity jet of conditioned gas entrains ambient air around it, delivering effective gas exchange with a tidal volume smaller than the patient’s anatomical dead space.

The Core Principle

From a single jet to
complete gas exchange

  • 1

    The Jet

    A longitudinal, coaxial cone of conditioned oxygen exits the jet catheter at high velocity, creating a low-pressure zone along the core stream.

  • 2

    Entrainment

    That pressure gradient draws ambient air — or bypass oxygen — in along the jet’s path, multiplying the effective delivered volume without enlarging the airway interface.

  • 3

    Exchange

    Gas exchange continues via Taylor-type dispersion, molecular diffusion, and facilitated diffusion — efficient even with tidal volumes below anatomical dead space.

  • 4

    Plug-and-Play Integration

    Air/oxygen blending, heated humidification, and live monitoring arrive pre-integrated — designed to drop into existing OR workflows without retraining.

Access Strategies

One ventilator,
three ways into the airway

Monsoon 4 supports the access techniques surgical teams already plan around — each suited to a different procedure type, airway status, and instrumentation need.

Supraglottic Jet Ventilation

A fine jet catheter runs through the laryngoscopy tube, its nozzle ending 3–4 cm from the tip — keeping the airway largely open for instrumentation and visualization.

Laryngoscopy-based ENT

Infraglottic Jet Ventilation

A narrow catheter with a central aperture and side ports delivers gas just below the glottis. Side wings secure to the neck; a Luer-Lock connector seats firmly into Monsoon 4.

Tumours & difficult airways

Superimposed Jet Ventilation

Monsoon 4 runs alongside a conventional ventilator via a jet adapter in place of the standard Y-piece — pulsing through both phases of the mandatory breath.

Combined conventional + jet
From Crate to Case-Ready

A workflow built to be
learned once, trusted every time

Monsoon 4’s setup and shutdown sequences are designed for plug-and-play operation — with or without active humidification.

01

Connect & power on

Link gas supplies, mains power, and the patient circuit. The 15″ ThoraDis touchscreen guides setup — no hidden menus.

02

Select configuration

Choose single or dual-jet mode, set frequency, inspiration timing, and pressure limits for the case at hand.

03

Add humidification

Engage the integrated heater/humidifier for longer cases requiring active conditioning of delivered gas.

04

Monitor & shut down

Live pressure, flow, and volume monitoring throughout the case, followed by a guided shutdown sequence.

In the Operating Room
